\## Problem Statement



Urban commuters face uncertainty because existing public transport systems provide schedules and basic tracking, without predicting delays, overcrowding, breakdowns, or route disruptions.



This can lead to longer travel times, missed connections, passenger inconvenience, and inefficient fleet management.



An AI-driven system is proposed to predict transport reliability and provide real-time decision support for both passengers and transport authorities.



\## System Overview



The Intelligent Public Transport Reliability \& Operations Management System is an AI-driven smart transportation platform designed to improve the reliability, efficiency, and predictability of urban public transport.



The system uses real-time transport information and intelligent prediction mechanisms to support passengers and transport authorities.



\## Core Functionalities



\- Real-time vehicle tracking

\- Estimated arrival time prediction

\- Delay prediction and reliability analysis

\- Overcrowding prediction and passenger load monitoring

\- Vehicle breakdown and disruption reporting

\- Alternative route recommendations

\- Route disruption alerts

\- Fleet monitoring

\- Operational dashboards

\- Performance and reliability reports

\- Notification and alert management
