\## Project Overview



The Intelligent Public Transport Reliability \& Operations Management System is an AI-driven smart transportation platform designed to improve the reliability, efficiency, and predictability of urban public transport.



Urban commuters often experience uncertainty because conventional public transport systems mainly provide scheduled timings and basic vehicle tracking. They generally do not predict delays, overcrowding, vehicle breakdowns, or route disruptions.



The proposed system uses real-time transport information and intelligent prediction mechanisms to support both passengers and transport authorities in making better decisions.



\## Core Functionalities



\- Real-time vehicle tracking

\- Estimated arrival time prediction

\- Delay prediction and reliability analysis

\- Overcrowding prediction and passenger load monitoring

\- Vehicle breakdown and disruption reporting

\- Alternative route recommendations

\- Route disruption alerts

\- Fleet monitoring

\- Operational dashboards

\- Performance and reliability reports

\- Notification and alert management
